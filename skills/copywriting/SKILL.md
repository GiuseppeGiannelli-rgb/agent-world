# Copywriting

## Before writing
Read the research notes: who the reader is, the problem they have, the promise of the product, proof that it works.

## Patterns
- **Headline**: the benefit in under 10 words. Then a sub-headline that says how.
- **Sections**: problem → solution → proof → objection handled → call to action.
- **Calls to action**: a verb plus the outcome ("Prenota il tuo tavolo"), never "Clicca qui".
- **Social hooks**: the first line must work alone; one idea per post.

## Voice
- Short sentences, concrete nouns, active verbs.
- Write in the language of the goal; keep the same register everywhere.
- Numbers and specifics beat adjectives.

## Deliver
One markdown file with a heading per section of the page or per post, ready to paste.
